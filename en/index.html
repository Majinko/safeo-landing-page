Why Excel and a calendar aren't enough

Tracking inspections in a spreadsheet works – until something slips

With dozens of units, different inspection types and multiple sites, keeping track of inspection deadlines becomes a risk. These are the situations Safeo ID solves.

Deadlines get lost in spreadsheets

Excel won't warn you that a professional inspection expires next month. You rely on one person's memory.

Every unit has different deadlines

Pressure, gas, lifting and electrical equipment each have their own inspection types and intervals. Looking them up every time eats up hours.

Reports are scattered

In emails, binders and shared drives. When an inspection or audit comes, you hunt for documents under pressure.

Defects have no owner

An inspection finds a defect, but it's unclear who should fix it and by when. It stays buried in a note.

FAQ

Questions about tracking inspections in Safeo ID

How does Safeo ID help determine inspection intervals?

For each unit, you choose the category (pressure, gas, lifting, electrical, fire), group and subgroup. When you add an inspection, the app suggests the inspection types for that category – for example a professional inspection, professional test or official test – and pre-fills their intervals. The “Valid until” date is calculated automatically.

You can adjust the interval at any time, and for some subgroups you enter it yourself. Pre-filled values are guidance only – the equipment operator is responsible for setting the deadlines, so we recommend confirming them with your inspector.

Does Safeo ID replace the inspector?

No. Inspections and professional checks are still carried out by an authorized person. Safeo ID helps you keep track of when each inspection needs to be ordered, what the result was, where the report is and who needs to fix any defects found.

When are deadline reminders sent, and to whom?

Email reminders go out 90, 30, 10, 5 and 1 day before an inspection, check, training or medical check-up expires. They go to the company's editors – and for trainings and medical check-ups, to the employee as well. When an inspection finds a defect, the responsible person gets an email right away.

What shows up when you scan the QR code on a unit?

A public equipment page with basic and technical details and a list of inspections – type, validity, responsible person and result. No login is required, so maintenance staff or an inspector can see the information right at the equipment.

What else can I track besides regulated technical equipment?

Employee trainings (with predefined types and intervals), medical check-ups, your vehicle fleet including roadworthiness tests, emissions checks, liability insurance and highway vignettes, plus custom items tailored to your operations.

Can I manage multiple companies and sites in one account?

Yes. You assign equipment, vehicles and employees to a company and site, and filter by them as well. Editors see only the companies they have access to.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Safeo ID is a web app that runs in the browser on a computer, tablet or mobile phone.
